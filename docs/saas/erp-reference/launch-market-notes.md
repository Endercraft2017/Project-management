## Jurisdiction, privacy and residency implications

- **Philippines:** the [Data Privacy Act, especially section21](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/), retains controller accountability when data is processed by third parties domestically or internationally and calls for contractual or other reasonable protection. Hosting overseas is not a waiver. Map controllers/processors, appoint responsibilities, evaluate NPC registration/DPO obligations against actual applicability, and define access, retention, breach handling and vendor contracts.
- **EU/EEA:** the [EDPB's GDPR basics](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-protection-basics_en) explicitly includes non-EEA organizations offering goods/services, even free, targeting EEA individuals. “We are in the PH” does not remove this analysis. Identify the lawful basis and controller/processor roles for accounts, tenant project data, support, analytics and AI; assess representative/DPA/data-subject-rights obligations with counsel.
- **Cross-border EU data:** [EDPB international-transfer guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/international-data-transfers_en) describes adequacy, appropriate safeguards such as SCCs and narrowly framed derogations. Philippines is not in the retrieved [European Commission adequacy list](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/adequacy-decisions_en). Assess data flows, vendor contracts and transfer risk rather than assuming consent or a provider's “GDPR compliant” badge resolves them.
- **UK:** evaluate UK GDPR separately. [ICO transfer guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-brief-guide-to-international-transfers/) describes adequacy, IDTA/Addendum and a transfer-risk assessment where safeguards are relied on. EU SCCs alone are not automatically the complete UK arrangement.
- **US:** obligations depend state, data and business thresholds. The [California AG CCPA guide](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) provides one relevant applicability test; do not claim every US visitor triggers the same statute or that US hosting alone satisfies privacy obligations. Exclude regulated-health/financial use at launch unless explicitly designed and reviewed.
- **Residency is a product/contract decision, not a blanket geographic rule:** targeting EU users does not by itself establish that every byte must be hosted only in the EU. Conversely, selecting an EU database does not make logs, email, storage, backups, PH support access, analytics, CDN or AI processing EU-only. Inventory all flows and choose one documented initial region, with explicit vendor residency/backup/support constraints. Do not promise regional isolation until verified end to end.

Practical launch defaults: minimal data collection, private tenant data, documented retention/export/deletion, no sensitive prompts/content in telemetry, optional analytics/replay disabled until privacy choices are implemented, contractual vendor review and a breach/restore playbook. Choose launch markets deliberately; adding UK/EU sales means more review than a PH-only pilot. International sales tax and privacy require qualified review; the notes above neither approve a jurisdiction nor certify compliance.
